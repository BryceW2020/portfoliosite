All about me!

I was born and raised in the beautiful state of Colorado, where I’ve spent my entire life surrounded by stunning mountains, wide-open plains, and all four seasons in their full glory. Growing up here has given me a deep appreciation for nature, adventure, and the simple joys of life—whether that’s hiking in the Rockies, enjoying a sunny day at a local park, or just taking in the fresh mountain air. When I’m not out exploring Colorado’s incredible scenery, you can usually find me hanging out with friends, working on cars, or playing video games. There’s nothing better than spending time with my closest friends, whether we’re just chilling, having a barbecue, or going on spontaneous road trips. Cars have always been a passion of mine—there’s something so satisfying about tinkering under the hood, fixing up an old engine, or just admiring a well-built machine. And when I need to unwind, I love diving into a good video game, whether it’s an intense multiplayer match or an immersive single-player story. Out of all the seasons, summer is definitely my favorite. I love the long, sunny days, the warm nights perfect for bonfires, and the endless opportunities for outdoor fun. From camping and fishing to cruising around with the windows down, summer in Colorado is unbeatable. Life here is never boring, and I wouldn’t trade my Colorado roots or my favorite hobbies for anything!
